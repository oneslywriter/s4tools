# S-4 MIDI CC reference

What every MIDI CC sets on the Torso S-4 (OS 2.x), across its whole 0-127 range, in the units the S-4 stores in a project ([project.json](S4-PROJECT.md)).

The CC numbers are the S-4's MIDI implementation, as Torso documents it. The ranges and curves were measured with s4tools: each CC was sent at known values in calibration projects, the S-4 saved the project, and its file was read back. The curves below reproduce every reading to float precision ([VERIFY.md](../VERIFY.md), calibration rounds 1-4).

[s4cc.json](s4cc.json) has every value: `parameters` holds one entry per row below, with `values`, the 128 stored values for CC 0 to 127 (or a `formula` where the value depends on the sample), and `scale_modes` holds each scale's 128 steps. Both files are generated by `tools/make_ccref.js` from the tables s4tools itself uses.

## Reading the tables

- **Channels:** a track's parameters on channels 1-4 (the track number), the mixer on 16, Perform on 15.
- **x = CC / 127.** The columns CC 0 ... CC 127 give the stored value at those CCs.
- **Modes:** a parameter's mode is stored next to its value (`{"value": ..., "mode": ...}`). A mode that changes what the CC means has its own row; modes listed together share the values.
- **Curves:**
  - lin a to b: a + (b - a) · x
  - expo a to b, k: a + (b - a) · (e^(k·x) - 1) / (e^k - 1), with fine steps at the low end
  - rexp a to b, k: b - (b - a) · (e^(k·(1 - x)) - 1) / (e^k - 1), with fine steps at the top
  - geo a to b: a · (b / a)^x
  - n steps: the knob moves evenly through a list of n values, value = list[round(x · (n - 1))]. The CC range of each step is listed under the table.
- **Note lengths** (rates and times in a synced mode) are stored as an index: 0 = 32/1, 1 = 16/1, 2 = 8/1, 3 = 4/1, 4 = 2/1, 5 = 1/1, 6 = 1/2, 7 = 1/4, 8 = 1/8, 9 = 1/16, 10 = 1/32, 11 = 1/64. TRIPLET and DOTTED keep the index and add T or D.
- **Scales** (RING's SCALE is the index): 0 CHROMATIC, 1 MAJOR, 2 MINOR, 3 DORIAN, 4 LYDIAN, 5 SUPER LOCRIAN, 6 MIXOLYDIAN, 7 HEX AEOLIAN, 8 HEX DORIAN, 9 BLUES, 10 PENTATONIC, 11 HIRAJOSHI, 12 KUMOI, 13 IWATO, 14 WHOLE TONE, 15 PELOG, 16 TETRATONIC, 17 FIFTHS, 18 OCTAVES, 19 FREE. A pitch or speed in a scale mode steps evenly through that scale's notes from -36 to +36 semitones.
- **Default:** the value a fresh device stores, and the CC nearest to it.
